How to get a useful result

A few details make a difference
🌿
1. Location

Search your village or district to check live weather. You can also use the location button if you choose to share your device location.

2. Soil values

Enter N, P, K and pH from a soil test if you have one. Sample values are prefilled to help you explore; replace them before using the report.

3. Budget and water

The report estimates costs and irrigation needs from project benchmarks. Check local input prices before making financial decisions.

Not an agronomist or price guarantee

Treat model output as decision support. Confirm sowing dates, soil measurements and market prices with your local agriculture officer or mandi.
